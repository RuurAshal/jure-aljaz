Where I fit best.

You are a young, fast-growing company, and you need someone who can do most of this job from day one and adapt the rest to how you work.

  • I have already done most of the work. Leading people, building performance and quality systems, training and developing teams, keeping processes audit-ready, and handling the difficult conversations, with advanced training in Reality Therapy.
  • I adapt to your situation. I have built things from scratch in five very different settings, from a club of 6 people to a global pharma company.
  • I am used to early-stage reality. Too few people, changing priorities and processes that are still taking shape.

Candidates with long careers in large companies often bring a ready-made playbook. That works when the processes already exist. In a young company it can be too rigid. I start by understanding how you actually work, then build the people processes around it.

How I work.

I have been a coach, a teacher, a co-founder and a manager in a regulated corporate setting. That mix shapes how I work on people and performance: hire for the stage the company is at, make expectations clear, give honest feedback early and keep the processes light enough that people actually use them.

My approach draws on Choice Theory, and I hold an advanced certificate in Reality Therapy, its practical method for honest, forward-looking conversations. The core idea: people do their best work when they understand why something matters and choose to own it. High performance comes from strong relationships and clear goals, not from pressure.
